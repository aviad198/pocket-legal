Privacy Policy

Pocket App  ·  Last updated: July 4, 2026

Pocket ("we", "our", or "us") is a mobile application that helps you organize and track loyalty cards and coupons. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you register, we collect your email address and a hashed password. We do not store your password in plain text.

Card and coupon data. Notes, codes, store names, expiry dates, and any images you add to your cards. This data is stored in your account and is not shared with other users.

Location data. If you use the store-search feature, we send a search query to Google Places API to return nearby results. We do not store your precise location on our servers.

Usage data. Anonymized events (e.g., screens visited, feature interactions) collected via PostHog to help us improve the app. No personally identifiable information is attached to these events.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Pocket service
  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure
  • Show nearby stores when you use the search feature
  • Analyze aggregate usage patterns to prioritize features

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services which have their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase – database and authentication hosting. Data is stored on EU-region servers.
  • Google Places API – store search and location lookup.
  • Google Gemini AI – optional AI-powered card scanning and analysis. Images submitted for analysis are processed by Google and are subject to Google's data-use policies.
  • PostHog – anonymized product analytics.

4. Data Retention

Your account and card data are retained as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time from within the app, which permanently removes your data from our systems within 30 days.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures including row-level security, encrypted connections (TLS), and hashed passwords. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

Pocket is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, use the in-app settings or contact us at the address below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via email or an in-app notice. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
