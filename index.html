How it works

Photograph the card. That's the whole setup.

  1. Snap a photo

    Point the camera at a card, a screenshot or a confirmation email. AI reads the store, code, balance and expiry date for you. No typing.

  2. Spend it at the till

    Show the barcode or QR code at checkout. Spent part of it? Log it and Pocket keeps the rest of the balance alive.

  3. Get a nudge before it expires

    Pocket reminds you 7, 3 and 1 day before a card runs out, so the money gets spent by you.

What's in your Pocket

  • ◉AI card capture

    One photo and the details are filled in: store name, code, value and expiry.

  • ₪Partial balances

    Spend ₪180 of a ₪200 card and Pocket keeps track of the ₪20 that's left, with a full transaction history.

  • ◷Expiry reminders

    A notification before anything expires, so you never find a dead card at the back of a drawer again.

  • ⌖Where to spend it

    A map of nearby shops that accept the cards you're already holding.

Pocket is independent. It isn't run by a card issuer or a bank, and it earns nothing when your card expires. Its only job is making sure you spend what's yours.
