Delete your Pocket account

Pocket App  ·  Last updated: July 7, 2026

You can permanently delete your Pocket account and all associated data at any time. This action is immediate and cannot be undone.

Option 1 – Delete from inside the app

  1. Open the Pocket app.
  2. Go to Settings (tap the gear icon or your profile).
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when prompted.

Your account is deleted immediately. All your data is permanently removed and cannot be recovered.

Option 2 – Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, send an email to aviad198@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account with the subject line "Account deletion request". We will process your request within 7 days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account and login credentials
  • All saved loyalty cards and coupons
  • All transaction history
  • All photos and images you uploaded

Data retention after deletion

Once deleted, your data is immediately and permanently removed from our systems. We do not retain personal data after account deletion, except for minimal records we are required to keep by applicable law. Any such retention is described in our Privacy Policy.
